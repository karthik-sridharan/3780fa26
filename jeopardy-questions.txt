CATEGORY: Supervised Learning & Model Selection
10) Name one way to choose the hyper-parameters of a learning algorithm.
20) [T/F] A classifier that gets 0% training error will have no more than 10% test error.
30) Why can you not choose $\lambda$ by minimizing the training error?
40) You train a model that gets low training error but high test error. Which way would you change each of the following, and why?<ol><li>$C$ in a regularized linear model</li><li>$k$ in $k$-nearest neighbours</li></ol>
50) You have 100 labelled points in total and 5 candidate values of $\lambda$. Describe exactly what 5-fold cross-validation does here: how many models do you fit, what gets averaged, and what do you do once $\lambda$ has been picked?

CATEGORY: k-NN & Perceptron
10) What is the main assumption behind nearest-neighbor classification?
20) What is the Perceptron update after a positive input point $\mathbf{x}$ is misclassified?
30) You use Euclidean distance in $d$ dimensions with $n$ training samples. How long does it take to classify one new test point with $k$-NN, in terms of $n$ and $d$?
40) In $k$-NN, if you set $k$ equal to the number of training examples $n$, what label does the classifier predict?
50) Assume you have points in 2D within the unit circle and a hyperplane that dissects the circle. As you add dimensions with random feature values, how are the pairwise point distances affected? How are the distances to the hyperplane affected?

CATEGORY: Linear Methods and ERM

20) Any $d$-dimensional linear classifier with a bias can be written as a <em>homogeneous</em> classifier in how many dimensions? Describe the transformation of $\mathbf{x}$ and of $\mathbf{w}$.
30) For each of the following, say whether the MLE solution can be written in closed form:<ol><li>Logistic regression</li><li>Least-squares regression</li></ol>
40) You model your data as $P(y_i\mid\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{w})=\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}_i^\top\mathbf{w},\sigma^2)$ and you have the prior $P(\mathbf{w})=\mathcal{N}(0,\eta^2 I)$. What training loss $\ell(\cdot,\cdot)$ and regularizer $R(\mathbf{w})$ does MAP estimation give you?
50) When would you prefer each of the following methods for solving linear regression?<ol><li>Closed-form solution</li><li>Gradient descent</li><li>Stochastic gradient descent</li></ol>
