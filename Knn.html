A coin-sized proof idea

  • C. Show that the mismatch error is at most twice the Bayes error.

PROOF WARM-UP

Two-draw mismatch

H
P(H) = 0.80
Bayes error0.200always predict the likelier side
two-draw mismatch0.320H→T or T→H

2p(1−p) ≤ 2(1−p) = 2 × Bayes error

Proof sketch: mismatch = p(1−p)+(1−p)p = 2p(1−p). If p ≥ 1/2, Bayes predicts heads and has error 1−p; therefore 2p(1−p) ≤ 2(1−p).
